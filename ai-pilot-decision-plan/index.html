1

Score four questions

Each question is scored 0 to 5 from evidence. The evidence and its source sit on the pilot's own tab, so anyone can check a score.
Value → value for money

What gets better than today, and do people use it?

  1. 1No better than today, or rarely used
  2. 3Some measured gain; some people use it
  3. 5Large measured gain; used every week
Cost → value for money

Is the full cost worth paying, including checking and rework?

  1. 1Costs more than it saves
  2. 3Roughly breaks even
  3. 5Clearly cheaper per task, all costs counted
Quality → readiness

Is the output good enough, checked by someone independent?

  1. 1Below the standard
  2. 3Meets the standard on some tasks
  3. 5Meets the standard on an independent check
Risk → readiness

If it goes wrong, is the harm small, and can someone stop it?

  1. 1Serious harm possible; nobody can stop it
  2. 3Controls exist but are untested
  3. 5Tested controls; a named owner can stop it

How to score

5 is always the good end, so a high Risk score means low risk.

No evidence scores 0. Use 2 or 4 when a pilot sits between two descriptions.

Scores are first drafts by the Digital Lead for SLT to challenge.

2

Add two totals and read the call

The two totals place a pilot on the map, and the zone it lands in is the rule-based call. The proposed call is the decision people make, and the dot shows it in its colour.
Value for money=Value+Cost Readiness to scale=Quality+Risk Each runs 0 to 10. 6 or more is high.
Set by the zone

Scale

Value for money 6+ and readiness 6+

Expand only the tasks and users the evidence supports, with a review date and a stop rule.

Moves to the next stage or the next tier.

Shrink

Value for money 6+, readiness 5 or less

Cut back tasks, users or authority. Run a bounded test of what is still uncertain.

Stays at its stage with a narrower scope or tier.

Kill

Value for money 5 or less

End this proposal. Name who closes it and keep what was learned.

Goes to Retire.
Overrides

Pause

A named blocker must clear first

Hold use until one blocker clears: a legal or safeguarding gate, an unsigned DPIA, missing evidence, or no capacity. Name an owner and a restart date, then re-score.

Holds at its stage. Pause is a hold, so it has no zone.

Red card

Quality or Risk at 0 or 1

No Scale, whatever the total.

Rule and judgement. The rule-based call is a formula. The proposed call is a judgement; when the two differ, the pilot says why.

Who owns it and who decides

Each pilot has one SLT sponsor who owns it and signs it off. Its forum takes the call at each review.

SLT

Money, priorities and college-wide sign-off. The Principal signs anything used college-wide.

Academic Board

Pedagogy and assessment: AI marking policy, coaching, the 4D frameworks and student AI tools. Led by the Deputy Headteacher Academic.
