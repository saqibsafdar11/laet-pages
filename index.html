A* Toolkit

188 classroom workflows using Google education tools, each tied to an A* discriminator, with separate routes for A, B and C students and a SEND adaptation. Includes exam-board criteria for 22 subject-board combinations and a cited evidence base.

Instructional coaching · filter by subject or tool · works offline

This site is public. Anything published here can be read by anyone with the link, and by anyone who finds it. robots.txt asks search engines not to index it, which is a request and not a control.

Nothing containing pupil or staff personal data, marks, safeguarding information, security audits or unpublished strategy belongs in this repository. Those stay in the private repositories.
